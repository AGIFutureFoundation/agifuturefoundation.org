The source for every name and number on this page is the April 2026 internal W3 series list: a three-page internal printout dated 2026-04-03, not a Wyoming Secretary of State filing. It names 33 sub-series, Series 1–33, contiguously. It proves no SOS filing, no legal designation, no bank account, no EIN, no intellectual property, and no Steward Council per series. The safe way to quote it, to a lawyer, a bank, or an investor, is exactly this: “33 named W3 sub-series in the April 2026 internal list.”

A note on the numbers you may see elsewhere. The live Foundation site lists a “66-W3 Wyoming Series network” and “33 agentic subsidiaries”, and other materials have mentioned 35 cells and 34 worlds. That numbering is site copy, not a filing, and the counts do not agree with each other. This hub follows the internal list — 33 named series — and flags the disagreement rather than picking the largest number. Until the 66 and 35 figures are explained or corrected with a document, do not quote them as fact.

The structure, in words

The intended structure has three tiers. At the top — the apex — sits the AGI Future Foundation, a filed California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation (Entity No. B20260196664, Articles filed 2026-04-24, SOS approval 2026-04-30, EIN assigned 2026-05-07). The Foundation’s role in the plan is governance and mission: holding the public-benefit purpose, the Fiduciary Shield, the Open Global Investment model, and the Windfall Clause described on the home page. In the middle, in the intended plan, sits W3 LLC, a Wyoming series LLC that would house the series as separate sub-series with isolated assets and liability. At the bottom sit the 33 series themselves — Cognition.X through InfoGurus — each intended to be DAO-managed day-to-day, with humans deciding, signing, and moving funds through a Steward Council.

Two cautions belong in the same passage as that description. First, only the top tier is verified as filed: the Foundation. W3 LLC formation and per-series designation are not verified in filings, so the middle and bottom tiers are intended, not established. Second, the Foundation as filed is a nonprofit. It cannot offer equity, a SAFE, or a profit share, and it cannot be owned or controlled by a for-profit head; control, if it ever exists, would be through written mission agreements, with the Foundation’s board remaining independent. Any for-profit benefit-corporation track discussed in the Foundation’s papers is a separate, proposed track that would need counsel to choose it in writing — it is not the filed nonprofit, and the two must not be presented as the same entity.

AGI Corp, named in the Foundation’s materials as an intended management company and venture studio, has an EIN only (assigned 2026-05-16). Its incorporation is unverified: an EIN alone does not prove incorporation, and no articles of incorporation for AGI Corp were in the files reviewed. The AGI Fellowship, linked from this hub, is a separate filed California nonprofit religious corporation (Entity No. B20260229217, Articles filed 2026-05-13, EIN assigned 2026-05-18). It is a partner, not a subsidiary, not a W3 series, and not part of this series list. No IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter is in the files for either the Foundation or the Fellowship, so no tax-exempt status and no tax-deductible donations are claimed anywhere on this site.

How to read the status labels

Intended / planned

The series is named in the April 2026 internal list and its domain is described on its page as a planned focus. No formation, funding, offering, team, product, or timeline is verified. Every series on this hub carries this label today.

Anchored (metaverse)

A metaverse fabric for the series is published on GitHub Pages with verified coordinates, but the assets it references are not hosted, so it does not render fully in Artemis yet. Four series are anchored: S01, S02, S03, and S08. Anchored is not the same as live.

No anchor yet

No metaverse fabric or coordinates are verified for the series. That is stated plainly on its page. A future fabric would need a published definition, hosted assets, and a verified render — and a live metaverse-server endpoint before anything could attach on RP1, where zero worlds are live today.

Every card links out

Each card below links to its series page. Pages for S01–S08 were built in this pass; pages for S09–S33 were built in a parallel pass in the same folder. Each page carries the same status banner and the same filing facts as this hub.

What “DAO-managed” means here. In the planned model, agents operate the day-to-day work of a series within limits set by humans, and a human Steward Council decides, signs, and moves funds. Agents never sign, move funds, hire or fire, offer securities, or enact doctrine. No Steward Council for any series is verified as seated, so no series page names one. Governance is described as a plan, not as an operating fact.
