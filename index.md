# Architecting Global AGI Prosperity

AGI Future Foundation is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation
(Entity No. B20260196664, Articles filed 2026-04-24, EIN assigned 2026-05-07),
based at 995 Market St FL 10, San Francisco, CA 94103. It is building an
agentic venture studio: agent teams do the day-to-day work under human-set
limits, and a human Steward Council decides. Agents never sign, move funds,
hire or fire, offer securities, or enact doctrine — humans execute, agents
prepare.

No IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter is in the files. No tax-exempt status
and no tax-deductible donations are claimed. Nothing on this site is an
offering: no SAFE, equity, price, valuation, cap, or deadline is stated as a
term.

## One interlocking system

Three mechanisms that only work together — legal protection, open ownership,
and a binding promise to share the windfall.

### The Fiduciary Shield

Expanded business-judgment protection for decisions that take the benefit
purpose into account. Not immunity.

### Open Global Investment

Broad economic participation separated from governance control. No terms
exist today.

### The Windfall Clause

A legal pre-commitment: profits above a threshold measured as a percentage of
global GDP are shared with humanity. Threshold, share, and administrator are
blank for counsel to set.
