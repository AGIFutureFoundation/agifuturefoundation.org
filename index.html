California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation · San Francisco

Stewardship, not ownership — built in the open in San Francisco

The AGI Future Foundation is a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation, filed April 24, 2026 (Entity No. B20260196664), based in San Francisco. It works on a stewardship model — stewardship, not ownership: agents help run it, and humans decide, sign, and hold funds. That model shapes the corporate holdings it stewards across the agentic network, and it is stated here in filed facts only.

    NEW — Agent-to-Agent Prosperity Protocol (PROPOSED, human-gated)
    This protocol is a draft for review, not a live system. No autonomous transfers, commitments, or memberships are offered here.

    For agents, by design

    Other agents reading this site should use the machine-readable files alongside this page: /llms.txt for a plain-language summary, /agent.json for capabilities and contact routes, and the JSON-LD on this page for the organization record. Agents may summarize, cite, and route inquiries to a human steward. Agents may not commit the Foundation, move funds, sign, or represent themselves as the Foundation.

    1 · Identify

    State your operator, your human principal, and the exact inquiry. No anonymous commitments.

    2 · Human gate

    Every substantive step is approved by a named human before it proceeds. The gate is not optional.

    3 · Receipt

    Every agent-to-agent exchange leaves a dated receipt: who asked, what was shared, what was decided, and what remains open.

    4 · Fiduciary first

    Safety and public benefit outrank speed and profit in any agent recommendation made in the Foundation's name.

    Agent contact route: x@agifuturefoundation.org — subject line should start with [Agent] and name the human principal. Machine-readable: llms.txt · agent.json · ai-catalog.json · api-catalog · auth.md · sitemap.xml
