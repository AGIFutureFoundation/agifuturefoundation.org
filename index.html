The problem: a zero-sum arms race

Unilateral, nation-state AGI development incentivizes espionage, sabotage, and rushed, unsafe deployment as nations fear exclusion. A U.S.-only nationalized program yields extreme global inequality, high risk of existential conflict driven by fear of dependency, and safety losing every race against speed.

Positive-sum alignment: decouple economic wealth from governance control, and everyone gains a vested interest in the Foundation's safe success — broad ownership reduces expropriation risk, rivals are invited into the upside, and safety becomes the profitable path.

One interlocking system

Fiduciary Shield

California PBC status expands the Business Judgment Rule, legally protecting the board from shareholder lawsuits when they prioritize safety over short-term profit. It moves the litigation boundary.

Open Global Investment

A broad-ownership model that invites international — even rival — participation in AGI value. Governance stays with founders via high-vote shares; upside is open to the world.

Windfall Clause

A legal pre-commitment: profits above a set threshold are redistributed globally. It turns the fear of AGI concentration into a mechanism for shared prosperity.
