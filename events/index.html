What this calendar is

This section is the Foundation’s single public calendar. It exists so that anyone who hears about BuilderSpace can check, in one place, what is actually scheduled, when it is, where it is (or whether that is still undecided), and how to reach the sign-up page for it. Every date, time, and link on these pages is taken from the event’s Luma listing, and where the Luma listing itself is incomplete or uses a placeholder, these pages say so instead of filling the gap with a guess.

That matters because a calendar is easy to make look finished. A time with an end attached looks confirmed; a city name next to a date looks booked; a cover image with a name and dates on it looks like the real event. None of those things is true here yet for the tour. The tour’s locations are TBD, some end times on Luma are placeholders the platform required, and two of the tour covers carry the wrong event name and dates in the image. This calendar records all of that plainly, on the same pages as the dates themselves, so nobody has to discover it later.

The calendar covers November 2026 through May 2027. It starts with onboarding, which is fully online and fully scheduled, and continues into the tour, which is scheduled in date and start time but not yet in place. Both tracks belong to the same programme, BuilderSpace, and both are hosted by Mike Rice. There are no other Foundation event series on this calendar: no separate conference, no fundraiser, and no launch event is listed here, because none is scheduled.

The BuilderSpace programme, in one paragraph

BuilderSpace is a hackathon tour bringing the San Francisco startup community to the Midwest — areas with new data centers and jobs — for SF founders, builders, hackers and startup teams plus local Midwest builders, students and talent, around building, shipping, hiring, and connecting SF startup energy with Midwest opportunity. It begins online, with five Thursday-evening onboarding sessions that introduce the tour, the community, and the work, and then it travels, with eleven events across January to May 2027: a season-opening kickoff, three multi-day build weekends, hands-on toolkit and skills days, pitch and community nights, and a Grand Finale on May 1, 2027. The point of the tour is the meeting itself — San Francisco builders working alongside Midwest builders, students, and local talent, shipping real projects in the places where new data-center and jobs growth is happening, and building the hiring and collaboration connections that outlast any single weekend.

Two things the programme is not: it is not a conference with a fixed venue and a published speaker list, and it is not a launch vehicle for a site or a property. No venues are confirmed, so no city is promised for any tour stop beyond the regional framing already given — Louisiana and the Midwest more broadly, as the event names suggest, but no specific venue, address, or city booking is claimed. No sponsors are confirmed either, so no sponsor is named on these pages, and no sponsor’s name, logo, or offer appears in connection with any event. And the Louisiana launch and any SmartCiti.X site launch are separate from this tour: the Grand Finale on May 1, 2027 closes the tour season, and it is not presented here as a site launch.

The two tracks

Track 1 · Onboarding — online, Nov–Dec 2026

Five Thursday evenings, 6:00–7:30pm Central (4:00–5:30pm Pacific), all online. Welcome to the Tour (Nov 12), Community Night (Nov 19), Build Night (Dec 3), Info Night on sponsors, training & incubators (Dec 10), and Pre-Season Prep (Dec 17). The onboarding track is the on-ramp: meet the community, ship a first project, learn what the tour offers, and arrive in January ready. Because every session is online, there is no venue question for this track at all.

The tracks are sequential by design: onboarding first, tour second. Someone who attends onboarding in November and December will know the people, the format, and the expectations before the first tour weekend in January. But onboarding attendance is not presented here as a requirement for the tour — no eligibility rule, application, or prerequisite is stated on Luma, and none is invented here. The Luma page for each event is the authoritative place to check what, if anything, an event asks of attendees.

How to use these pages

  • Calendar — month-by-month grids from November 2026 through May 2027, plus a chronological list of all 16 events with full details and Luma links.
  • About BuilderSpace — the programme in full: who it is for, what happens at the events, and what it is not.
  • Onboarding — the five online sessions, November 12 – December 17, 2026.
  • Tour — the eleven tour events, January 8 – May 1, 2027.
  • Itinerary — the detailed schedule: every event in order, with its date, time, format, registration link, and description as listed on Luma, plus the four legs and how the tour moves.
  • Call for Sponsors — the invitation to sponsor the tour. No tier, price, or benefit is set yet; terms are agreed by direct conversation.

Each event listing gives the event name, the date, the time in Central time (with the Pacific equivalent for the online onboarding sessions), whether it is online or location TBD, and a link to its Luma page. The Luma page is the sign-up route: because every event is Private and unlisted, the link is the way in. There is no public Luma listing to search for, no invite list that has gone out, and no other sign-up route offered here.

Times are given as listed on Luma, in Central time, because the tour takes place in the Central time zone and the onboarding sessions were listed that way. The onboarding sessions also carry their Pacific equivalents, 4:00–5:30pm, because the San Francisco community they are aimed at is on Pacific time.

What’s not decided yet

The honest version of this calendar includes its gaps. The following are not decided, and are not presented as decided anywhere on these pages:

  • Venues. No venue is confirmed for any of the eleven tour events. Locations are listed as TBD, and no city, venue name, address, or booking is claimed.
  • Sponsors. No sponsors are confirmed. No sponsor is named, and no sponsor offer is described. The December 10 Info Night — titled “Sponsors, Training & Incubators” — is a session about those topics; its title does not mean sponsors have been secured. See the Call for Sponsors.
  • Final end times. End times are placeholders where Luma required one. The three multi-day hackathons show the start time as the end time, and the two 10:30pm meetups show 11:30pm as the end. On those five events, the end time is TBD.
  • Agendas. No detailed agenda is published for any event beyond its name, date, time, and format as listed on Luma. No speaker, judge, mentor, workshop, or session is named, because none is confirmed on the listings.
  • Attendance. No registration counts and no attendee numbers are given anywhere on these pages, because none is published. Nothing here asks anyone to hurry, and there is no “join now” urgency: the events are unlisted, the links are the way in, and the dates are months out.

Two things to know before you share a link

Cover-image error on two tour events. The Luma covers for the Cajun Code Sprint (March 5–7, 2027) and the Prairie AgTech Build Weekend (March 26–28, 2027) show Bayou’s name and the January 9–11 dates in the image. That is a known cover error: those are not Bayou’s dates, and Bayou is a different weekend — the Bayou AI Build Weekend, January 9–11, 2027. The covers should be fixed on Luma before any public use, and the dates on these pages — March 5–7 for Cajun Code Sprint and March 26–28 for Prairie AgTech — are the dates as listed on Luma, not the dates in the cover images. Do not describe the covers as correct.

The Grand Finale is not a site launch. The tour ends with the Grand Finale on Saturday, May 1, 2027, 1:00–5:00pm Central. It closes the BuilderSpace season. The Louisiana launch and any SmartCiti.X site launch are separate undertakings and are not tour stops; nothing on these pages presents the Grand Finale, or any other tour event, as a site launch, a property opening, or a real-estate event.
