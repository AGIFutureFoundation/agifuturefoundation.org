The four legs of the tour

The tour moves in four legs. The regions below are the tour’s geography — Louisiana and Nebraska, in regions with new data centers and jobs — as the Luma pages describe it. They are not venue promises: no city, venue, or site is named for any leg until it is confirmed.

Leg 1 — Onboarding, online (November 12 – December 17, 2026)

Five Thursday evenings, online, 6:00–7:30pm Central. This is where builders meet the tour, form teams, ship a first project, learn about the public benefit corporation behind it, and get ready for January. Nothing to travel to: every onboarding night is online, and the Luma link is the sign-up.

Leg 2 — Louisiana: the season opening (January 8 – February 17, 2027)

The tour opens in person with the Season Opening Kickoff on Friday, January 8, followed immediately by the Bayou AI Build Weekend, January 9–11. After a four-week gap, the leg continues with the Crescent City Founder Toolkit on Thursday, February 11, and the NOLA Builder Night on Wednesday, February 17. Locations to be announced for all four stops.

Leg 3 — Nebraska and the Midwest (February 18 – April 22, 2027)

The tour moves on the day after the NOLA Builder Night: Heartland Builders & Coffee on Thursday, February 18. After a two-week gap comes the Cajun Code Sprint, March 5–7, then the Prairie AgTech Build Weekend, March 26–28, the Prairie Builder Night on Thursday, April 8, the Red River Pitch Night on Thursday, April 15, and BuildFest Skills & Certs Day on Thursday, April 22. Locations to be announced for all seven stops.

Leg 4 — Grand Finale (May 1, 2027)

Nine days after BuildFest, the tour closes with the Grand Finale on Saturday, May 1, 1:00–5:00pm Central — a closing demo day where the Midwest shows what it built. Location to be announced. The Grand Finale is not a SmartCiti.X site launch.

How the tour moves

  • Online first, then on the road. Five weeks of online onboarding (November–December) come before any travel.
  • Back-to-back opening weekend. The kickoff (January 8) runs straight into the Bayou AI Build Weekend (January 9–11) — one trip covers both.
  • Gaps between stops. The tour does not run every weekend: there are gaps of roughly one to four weeks between most stops, and a nine-day gap between BuildFest (April 22) and the Grand Finale (May 1).
  • Do not book travel yet. No venue is confirmed for any stop. Book nothing — no flights, no hotels, no rental cars — until a venue is announced for the stop you plan to attend.
  • What to bring is decided at Pre-Season Prep. The December 17 onboarding night covers what to bring and how tour stops work; until then, nothing is promised about equipment, lodging, or transport.
