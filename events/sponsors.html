Call for Sponsors

BuilderSpace is looking for sponsors for its 16-event hackathon tour, November 2026 to May 2027 — a tour bringing San Francisco startup teams to the Midwest, to train and help local economies in regions with new data centers and jobs. If your organization wants to support that, we want to hear from you.

What a sponsor would support

BuilderSpace is a hackathon tour run by the AGI Future Foundation, a filed California nonprofit public benefit corporation. The tour brings San Francisco startup teams to Louisiana and Nebraska, in regions with new data centers and jobs, to train and help local economies — not just to build and leave. The season runs from the first onboarding night on November 12, 2026 to the Grand Finale on May 1, 2027, and includes three build weekends (the Bayou AI Build Weekend, the Cajun Code Sprint, and the Prairie AgTech Build Weekend), two builder nights, a pitch night, a skills day, workshops, and meetups. The full schedule is on the itinerary page.

A sponsor supports the tour itself — the programme of 16 events and the builders who take part in it. What that support looks like in practice — and what a sponsor receives for it — is not fixed yet. It is agreed by direct conversation, event by event or for the season as a whole, and only once agreed is it written down and published here.

What is not promised

So that no one is misled, this page promises none of the following, because none of it is confirmed:

  • No tier, price, or package. There is no bronze, silver, gold, or named tier, and no published price for any level of support.
  • No logo placement, booth, talk, or naming right. No visibility term, venue placement, or naming is promised, because no venue is confirmed and no sponsor agreement exists yet.
  • No attendee count. No attendance figure is published for any event, and none is promised.
  • No investment terms. The tour’s Luma pages describe plans to invest in manufacturing and buildings to host incubator programs, with investment amounts, building sites, partners, and timelines still TBD. Sponsorship of the tour is not an investment in any company, building, or site, and this page makes no investment offer.
  • No tax statement. The Foundation is a filed nonprofit public benefit corporation; no IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter is in hand, and no sponsorship payment is claimed tax-deductible here.

How to become a sponsor

Email x@agifuturefoundation.org and say which event or part of the season you would like to support, and what you would like in return. The Foundation will reply, and any terms agreed will be written down and published on this page once they exist. Until then, this page stays as it is: an invitation, not a price list.

The December 10 onboarding night — Info Night: Sponsors, Training & Incubators — is a session about sponsors, training, and incubators. It is not itself a sponsor commitment, and attending it does not make an organization a sponsor.
