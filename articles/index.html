Status — please read first:
Every series named here comes from the April 2026 internal W3 series list: 33 named sub-series, Series 1–33. The AGI Fellowship (Article 44) is a separate filed church and a partner, not a series and not a subsidiary. Nothing on this hub, or in any article it links to, is an offer to sell securities, an invitation to invest, or a solicitation of funds: there are no investment terms here, because there is nothing to invest in yet.

How the word counts are measured. Every article is 2,000–2,500 words and every category hub is 1,200–1,800 words, counted the same way throughout: the words inside the page’s <main> element, with HTML tags stripped and entities decoded. The count beside each article below is that count, taken from the file as built on 2026-11-05. The total across all 44 articles is 106,690 words by the same method. If a future edit changes an article, its count should be re-run the same way and updated here.

How to read the thin articles. Only 8 of the 33 series have any repository coverage in the Foundation’s GitHub organisation (56 repos reviewed 2026-11-05), and most series are verified by name and number only. Their articles say so honestly and focus on the DAO and stewardship model and the open questions, rather than inventing detail. Where real code exists — Cognition.X, Locator.X, SmartCiti.X, the Biotech tool, Sentinel Stream, WorldForge, Metropolis-SmartCiti on Monad testnet — the article links to the repository and says what it contains. Where a landing page is only a stub, or a repo is an empty shell, the article says that too.
