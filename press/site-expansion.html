AGI Future Foundation Prepares Expanded Website Covering Its 33 Planned W3 Series, Governance, and Operations

New locally built pages add series landing pages, project pages, governance and operations sections, and an information-only investors section, pending a deploy decision

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 5, 2026 — The AGI Future Foundation has prepared a major expansion of its website, building new pages that describe its planned work in one place. The expansion was built locally and has not been deployed. The Foundation's live site, at https://www.agifuturefoundation.org, is hosted by Kylon, and the decision on deploying the new pages has not yet been made.

The expansion adds landing pages for all 33 named W3 series, Series 1 through Series 33, drawn from the Foundation's April 2026 internal list. The series run from Series 1, Cognition.X, through Series 33, InfoGurus. Each series page carries a status banner stating that the series is intended or planned, and not formed, funded, or offered. No series described in the expansion has been presented as an operating company, and no offering has been made in connection with any of them.

The expansion also adds 11 project pages. The pages cover WorldForge, Metropolis, Shield, Insidr, trueforge, Sentinel, PlaudGuard, Dyva, the RP1 ecosystem, AGI Corp, and the AGI Fellowship as a partner. The project pages describe the work as planned or in development, and do not present any project as completed, funded, or available where that is not the case.

A new six-page Governance section sets out the structures the Foundation has described for its work. It includes the Fiduciary Shield, described as expanded business-judgment protection for decisions that take the Foundation's benefit purpose into account, not as immunity from liability. It also includes the Windfall Clause, described as a pre-commitment on future windfall, with the threshold left blank for counsel to set. The section further describes share classes A, B, and C, and the Steward Council.

A five-page Operations section describes how the Foundation's work is organised. It covers eight agent roles, the venture studio model, the proposal flow, and the human gate under which people, not agents, make decisions on signing, funds, hiring, and other matters reserved to humans. A Get Involved section has also been built, alongside the governance and operations material.

The expansion includes a six-page Investors section. Every page in that section carries an "Information only — not an offer" banner. Nothing has been issued under the material described there, and no investment is being offered. Any suggested valuations in the material are illustrative only, and no figures from different entities or series should be added together into a combined total. The section is intended as background information, not as solicitation.

The expansion also includes a videos section with 40 videos in the library. The videos are AI-generated illustrations. They are not footage of real factories, events, or dashboards, and should not be read as documentation of physical sites or operating results.
