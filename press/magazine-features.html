AGI Future Foundation Publishes First Eleven Magazine Features

The Wave, Biotech Swarm, Flow Zone, and eight more long-form essays are now live on the Foundation's GitHub Pages site

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — The AGI Future Foundation has published its first eleven magazine features, numbered 01 through 11, on its website. The features are live on the Foundation's GitHub Pages site following the Nov. 6, 2026 deploy; the www.agifuturefoundation.org cutover is pending, and this release must not go out until the cutover is confirmed.

The eleven features are "The Wave," "Biotech Swarm," "Flow Zone," "Trade Craft Academy," "Physical AI Governors," "Worlds on Real Maps," "Shield and Windfall," "The Watchers," "Agent Economy," "Agents Operate, People Govern," and "Agentic Venture Studio." Each is a long-form essay written in a magazine style for general readers, setting out one part of the Foundation's work in plain language.

The set opens with the forces the Foundation is responding to. "The Wave" describes the arrival of capable AI systems. "Biotech Swarm" follows the life sciences, and "Flow Zone" takes up the experience of working inside systems that move faster than their operators. "Trade Craft Academy" turns to the skilled trades and how they change when machines join the work, and "Physical AI Governors" describes the governance of AI in physical machines.

The remaining features describe the Foundation's own model. "Worlds on Real Maps" covers the metaverse work built on real geography. "Shield and Windfall" sets out the Fiduciary Shield and the Windfall Clause, the two protections at the center of the Foundation's governance. "The Watchers" describes oversight, "Agent Economy" describes the economy agents work inside, "Agents Operate, People Govern" states the rule that agents operate day-to-day while people govern, and "Agentic Venture Studio" describes the venture studio model under which new projects are built.

Nothing in the features is an offering. No series, company, or project described in them is presented as formed, funded, or available where that is not the case. The Fiduciary Shield is described as expanded business-judgment protection for decisions that take the Foundation's benefit purpose into account, not as immunity from liability, and the Windfall Clause is described with its threshold left blank for counsel to set. Any suggested valuations in the Foundation's material are illustrative only, and figures from different entities or series should not be added together into a combined total.
