AGI Future Foundation Builds 44-Article Deep Library Covering Every Series and Project

One article per series and project, 106,690 words in eight categories, each carrying the same status banner, model, and open questions

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — The AGI Future Foundation has built a 44-article deep library covering every one of its planned W3 series and projects, one article per series or project, totalling 106,690 words. The library is live in the Foundation's GitHub Pages repository (verified by the coordinator's file-by-file sync, 2026-11-06). www.agifuturefoundation.org does not yet serve the new site, so this release must not go out until the www cutover is confirmed.

The 44 articles are organized in eight categories. Intelligence and Mind covers Cognition.X, Empathic.X, and InfoGurus. Health and Life Sciences covers ten series, from Diagnostic.X through Genome.Blockchain. Metaverse and Spatial covers Locator.X, Avatar.Chat DAO, Metaverse DAO, WorldForge, and the RP1 ecosystem. Robotics and Physical AI covers Get.Delivery DAO, Robotics DAO, and Aerospace DAO. Governance and Law covers Blockchain, RomanBridge.DAO, CMMC, the Fiduciary Corporate Shield, Insidr W3LLC, Sentinel Stream, and PlaudGuard-AI. Finance and the Studio covers the Incubator DAO, trueforge, Dyva, and AGI Corp. Cities, Energy, and Infrastructure covers SmartCiti.X, ParkingSmart.X, Solar DAO, and Metropolis-SmartCiti. Community, Learning, and Culture covers eight series and projects, ending with the AGI Fellowship Church as a partner.

Every article follows the same shape. Each carries a status banner stating that the series or project is intended or planned, and not formed, funded, or offered. Each carries a short summary, the DAO and stewardship model for that series, verified metaverse facts only, a plain account of what exists today against what is planned, how the series fits the Foundation's mission, open questions, and a box written for agents. Nothing in the library is an offering, and no article presents a planned series as an operating company.

The library is written to be read two ways: a reader can follow one series from its article alone, or read across a category to see how the series in that theme relate. Word counts were measured inside the main text of each article, by the same method throughout, and the total of 106,690 words is the sum of those counts. The library does not change the Foundation's filed status, and it makes no claim about tax exemption. Suggested valuations in the Foundation's material are illustrative only, and figures from different entities or series should not be added together into a combined total.
