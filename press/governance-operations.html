AGI Future Foundation Publishes Governance and Operations Libraries

Six governance pages and five operations pages set out the Shield, the Windfall Clause, share classes, the studio model, and the stage gates

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — The AGI Future Foundation has published its Governance and Operations libraries on its website: six governance pages and five operations pages setting out how the Foundation's work is governed and how it is run. The libraries are live on the Foundation's GitHub Pages site following the Nov. 6, 2026 deploy; the www.agifuturefoundation.org cutover is pending, and this release must not go out until the cutover is confirmed.

The six-page Governance section covers the Fiduciary Shield, the Windfall Clause, the share structure, the Steward Council, and Open Global Investment. The Fiduciary Shield is described as expanded business-judgment protection for decisions that take the Foundation's benefit purpose into account. It is not immunity from liability, and this release does not describe it as such. The Windfall Clause is described as a pre-commitment on future windfall, with the threshold left blank for counsel to set — the clause is a framework, not a finished term.

The Governance section also describes the Foundation's share classes. Class A carries one vote and full economics, Class B carries ten votes for aligned partners, and Class C carries one thousand votes and is held by the Foundation alone, for control only. The Steward Council is described as the body that watches over the Foundation's work, and Open Global Investment is described as a framework for future participation, with no terms set today.

The five-page Operations section describes how the work is organised day to day. It covers the eight agent roles, the human gate under which people, not agents, make decisions on signing, funds, hiring, and other matters reserved to humans, the proposal flow, and the venture studio model. The studio model runs in two modes: agent-managed, where agents run day-to-day work within human-set limits, and agent-assisted, where humans do the work with agents as co-workers. The section also sets out the stage gates, from Idea through Validate, Build, Launch, and Operate or Scale, to Exit or Wind-down.

Nothing in either library is an offering, and nothing has been issued under the material described there. The libraries are background information about how the Foundation intends its work to be governed and run, not solicitation, and any suggested valuations in the Foundation's material are illustrative only.
