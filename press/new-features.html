The second set of long-form essays covers the mission gate, the Steward Council, the long money, the first company, the cohort machine, and the windfall promise

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — The AGI Future Foundation is writing ten more magazine-style features, numbered 12 through 21, continuing the feature series that began with features 01 through 11. The new features are being written in parallel and are not yet live on the Foundation's website. They will be published only after Mike approves the exact version, and no publication date has been set.

The ten new features are "The Mission Gate," "The Steward Council," "The Open Door," "The Long Money," "The Builders," "The First Company," "The Fellowship Partnership," "The Cohort Machine," "The Windfall Promise," and "The Proof Is the Product." Each is planned as a long-form essay of roughly 4,000 to 5,000 words, written in a magazine style for general readers, in the same form as the first eleven features.

The set opens with the structures that govern the Foundation's work. "The Mission Gate" describes the gate through which new work must pass before it begins. "The Steward Council" describes the council that watches over that work. "The Open Door" describes who the Foundation's work is open to, and on what terms. "The Long Money" takes up the question of funding work whose results may be years away.

The remaining features turn to the work itself. "The Builders" describes the people and agents building the Foundation's projects. "The First Company" follows the first company built under the Foundation's model. "The Fellowship Partnership" describes the Foundation's partnership with the AGI Fellowship Church, which is a separate church, not a subsidiary. "The Cohort Machine" describes how cohorts of new projects are formed. "The Windfall Promise" describes the Foundation's pre-commitment on future windfall, with its threshold left blank for counsel to set. "The Proof Is the Product" closes the set on the standard the Foundation sets for itself: that the work must be shown, not only described.

Nothing in the features is an offering. No series, company, or project described in them is presented as formed, funded, or available where that is not the case, and any suggested valuations in the Foundation's material are illustrative only. The features are essays, not legal documents, and they do not change the Foundation's filed status or its governance.
