Press Release — November 5, 2026

AGI Future Foundation Prepares Events Calendar for BuilderSpace Onboarding and Tour

New section organizes 16 BuilderSpace events from November 2026 through May 2027, pending a deploy decision

Filed status. The AGI Future Foundation is a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation, Entity No. B20260196664, with Articles filed on 2026-04-24 and an Employer Identification Number assigned on 2026-05-07. The Foundation has not received an IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter. No claim of tax-exempt status or tax deductibility is made here.

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 5, 2026 — The AGI Future Foundation has prepared a new Events section for its website, organizing the BuilderSpace programme into a single calendar covering November 2026 through May 2027. The section is built locally and has not been deployed. No deploy date has been set, and the section is not live, launched, or available on the Foundation's website at this time.

The prepared section brings together 16 BuilderSpace events listed on Luma: five online onboarding nights and 11 tour events. It is intended to give visitors one place to find the programme's schedule. Whether and when it appears on the live site remains a deploy decision for the Foundation.

BuilderSpace is a hackathon tour bringing the San Francisco startup community to the Midwest, to areas with new data centers and jobs. The Events section presents the programme in two tracks: the online onboarding nights that precede the tour, and the tour itself, which runs from January through the start of May 2027.

The onboarding track consists of five online events held on Thursdays from 6:00 to 7:30 p.m. Central, from Nov. 12 through Dec. 17, 2026. They are the programme's opening track, scheduled before the tour events begin in January. The prepared calendar lists each onboarding night with its date and time, and each event page embeds its live Luma card.

The tour track consists of 11 events scheduled from Jan. 8 through May 1, 2027. Locations for tour events are to be determined unless an event is marked online. No venues are confirmed for the tour events, and no sponsors are confirmed. The prepared section does not present a venue or sponsor for any tour event.

All 16 events are Private and unlisted on Luma. They are link-reachable and are not publicly listed, and no invites have been sent. That status is unchanged by the preparation of the Events section. The section organizes links to the existing Luma event pages; it does not change how the events are listed on Luma, and it does not make a Private event public.

Each event page in the prepared section embeds its live Luma card — a real embed of the real Luma event page for that event. Because the section is not deployed, that navigation is not currently available on the Foundation's website.

The tour concludes with the Grand Finale on May 1, 2027. The Grand Finale is the closing event of the tour track and the final date on the prepared calendar. It is not a SmartCiti.X site launch. The prepared section does not describe the Grand Finale as a site launch, and no site, address, or launch location is named for it.

The Foundation's live website remains at https://www.agifuturefoundation.org, where it is hosted by Kylon. The Events section described here is separate from that live site: it exists as a local build only. A decision to deploy it would place the calendar and event pages on the site; until such a decision is made and carried out, the live site does not include this section. The Foundation is not announcing a launch date for the section in this release.
