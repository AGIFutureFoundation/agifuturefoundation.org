AGI Future Foundation Prepares Video Library and YouTube Channel Embed for Its Website

Forty AI-generated videos prepared for the Foundation site, with the live AGI Corp YouTube channel embedded through its uploads playlist, pending a deploy decision

Filed status. The Foundation has not received an IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter. No claim of tax-exempt status or tax deductibility is made here.

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 5, 2026 — The AGI Future Foundation has prepared a video library of 40 videos for its website, along with an embed of the AGI Corp YouTube channel, @AGI-Corp, through the channel's uploads playlist. The work has been built locally and is pending a deploy decision; it has not yet been published to the Foundation's website.

The Foundation's website, https://www.agifuturefoundation.org, is live and Kylon-hosted. The new videos page and the YouTube embed prepared for the site are separate from that live site and remain local only until a deploy decision is made. The release describes work prepared for the site, not a page that visitors can already open there.

The 40 videos in the prepared library are AI-generated illustrations. They are not footage of real factories, events, or dashboards, and they should not be understood as recordings of Foundation operations, facilities, or results. The YouTube embed, by contrast, presents the live channel's own uploads: the AGI Corp YouTube channel, @AGI-Corp, is live at https://www.youtube.com/@AGI-Corp, and the prepared embed draws on that channel's uploads playlist.

The Foundation prepared the library and the channel embed as a single addition to its website, so that visitors who reach the site will be able to find the Foundation's prepared videos and the live channel's uploads in one place once the work is deployed. Until that deploy decision is made, the prepared pages remain local only, and the live website continues as it is today. No launch date for the new pages is stated in this release, and no other change to the website is described here.

This release does not list individual video titles, and it does not include view counts or subscriber numbers for the channel. Those details were not verified for this release, and they are not part of the announcement. The announcement is limited to the prepared library, the prepared embed, and the status of that work: built locally, pending a deploy decision, and not yet published to the Foundation website. Anyone seeking the channel in the meantime can visit the live AGI Corp YouTube channel directly at https://www.youtube.com/@AGI-Corp, while the Foundation website remains at https://www.agifuturefoundation.org.

The AGI Future Foundation is a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation, Entity No. B20260196664, with Articles filed on 2026-04-24 and an Employer Identification Number assigned on 2026-05-07.
