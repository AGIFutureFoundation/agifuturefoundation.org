Structure at a glance

Solid borders mark what is filed and verified. Dashed borders mark what is intended or proposed and not formed. The intended structure is a plan, not a completed fact: control between separate entities would rest on written mission agreements, not ownership, and no intended entity below is a subsidiary of the Foundation today.
