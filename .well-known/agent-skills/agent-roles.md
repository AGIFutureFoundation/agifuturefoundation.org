## The eight roles

Every series is staffed by the same eight agent roles — functions, not
people and not owners — working under a human Steward Council, inside
written limits:

1. **Research synthesis** — gather material and turn it into a sourced
   synthesis a human can decide on. Does not decide which ideas enter
   validation; does not present agent research as human interviews.
2. **Task routing** — sort incoming work and send it to the role (or
   human) that should handle it. Any task needing a signature, bank
   transfer, filing, hire, securities offer, or doctrinal act routes
   to a human, full stop.
3. **Proposal drafting** — write proposals in a defined shape (what,
   why, cost, changes, risks, decision asked for) for the Council to
   approve, amend, or decline. The drafter never votes or counts a
   vote; a draft is labelled a draft until a human adopts it.
4. **Coordination** — keep people, tasks, and timing lined up.
   Coordination is not authority: it cannot approve a budget, reassign
   a human's responsibilities, hire or fire, or commit the series.
5. **Progress tracking** — maintain the record of what actually
   happened; feeds the portfolio dashboard. Actuals only — no
   projection in a revenue or funds column, no unsigned partner,
   customer, or investor listed, and an agent readiness flag is never
   a gate pass.
6. **Monitoring** — watch operations, support queues, analytics, risks,
   and mission/compliance items; flag and report with evidence. Does
   not act beyond the written limits the Council set.
7. **Collaborator scouting** — find candidate researchers, builders,
   communities, partners, and customers and bring them to a human.
   A scouted collaborator is not a partner until a human with
   authority signs the agreement. Agents do not solicit investment.
8. **Grant drafting** — write grant drafts for a human to review,
   correct, and submit; never sent under an agent's name or authority.
   A grant draft must not promise equity, returns, or a profit share,
   and must not present the Foundation as tax-exempt or donations as
   tax-deductible.

## The coordinator — covering unfilled roles

The coordinator is not a ninth role; it is the rule for an empty role.
When a role is unfilled, a named coordinator covers that role's work
inside that role's limits, and the coverage is recorded/minuted until
the role is filled.

## Identity, turnover, and the record

Agent identity is recorded — name, model, and instance per role — so a
proposal traces to the agent that drafted it and the human that adopted
it. Roles are functions: when an agent or model changes, the assignment
is updated and minuted; a new agent inherits the work description only,
never a human's signing authority.

## Hard limits — the same for all eight roles

Agents NEVER: sign a legal document; move funds (default agent spending
limit is $0); hire or fire; offer securities; enact doctrine or charter
changes; represent the Foundation or a series to regulators, banks, or
the public as a decision-maker; or hold equity, tokens, or profit
interests. These limits apply in both studio modes (agent-managed and
agent-assisted). Any task needing a signature, a bank transfer, or a
filing is human-only by definition.
