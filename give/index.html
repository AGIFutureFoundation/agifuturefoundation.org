Our story, in filed facts

A San Francisco foundation, built in the open

The AGI Future Foundation is a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation. Its articles were filed on April 24, 2026, and it is based in San Francisco.

From the start, the Foundation has published its work in the open: research, articles and guides are published free and open on this site, so the work can be read and used without a paywall.
