What the channel is

AGI is the video channel of the AGI Future Foundation and AGI Corp — the same family of projects this site describes. The channel is where the family's videos live in public: explainers, overviews, and films about the Foundation's mission, its structure, and the series it plans to build. The channel's name on YouTube is AGI, and its handle is @AGI-Corp.

The channel is a public window into work in progress. The Foundation's plans are exactly that — plans. The W3 series are intended and planned, not formed, funded, or offered; the SmartCiti.X corporation is proposed, not formed; nothing has been issued. A video about a plan is a video about a plan, and it should be watched that way: as an explanation of an idea, not as evidence that the idea has already happened.

Two kinds of video on this site

It is worth saying plainly, because the two look alike at a glance. The 40 videos on this site's videos hub — the explainers, the series shorts, the deep dives, the spotlights — are AI-generated campaign illustrations. They show imagined scenes, not real factories, events, dashboards, or results, and every one of them carries that caption. The YouTube channel is different in kind: it is the live channel, and its uploads are the channel's own uploads. This page does not re-label the channel's uploads as illustrations, and it does not claim they are footage of anything either — the playlist is presented as it stands, and each upload speaks for itself.

That distinction matters for how to use this page. If you want the Foundation's written record — the filed facts, the governance, the honest status of every series — read the site: the home page, governance, and the series pages. If you want to watch, watch the playlist above or the videos hub. Neither replaces the other.

What this page does not say

Three things are deliberately absent, and their absence is the point. First, no video titles are listed here. The titles of individual uploads were not verified from the channel page, so none are reproduced — the playlist embed is the whole video surface, and it shows the titles itself. Second, no view counts. Third, no subscriber numbers. A channel's size is not a claim this site needs to make, and it is not one it has verified. If you want those numbers, YouTube shows them on the channel page.

The same honesty applies to what the channel is for. It is not an offering. Nothing on this page invites an investment, a purchase, or a payment. The Foundation's fundraising is donation only, and no donation is taken on this page — anyone who wants to give or to get involved writes to x@agifuturefoundation.org and talks to a human first. The channel is a way to watch and to follow, not a way to buy in.

Where to start watching

If you are new here, the shortest route in is the site's own videos hub. Start with the four explainers: the Fiduciary Shield, Open Global Investment, the Windfall Clause, and Diagnostic.X. Each is a few minutes long, each is captioned as an AI-generated illustration, and each links to the written page behind the idea — the Shield to governance, the series films to the series pages. From there, the deep dives run longer, and the spotlights go narrower — SmartCiti.X, Robotics, the holodeck. The playlist above is the other half of the picture: the channel's own uploads, in order, newest first. Between the two, the hub explains the ideas and the channel shows the family's latest films; the written pages carry the record of what is filed, what is planned, and what is not yet true.

The events calendar is a third way in. The Foundation's BuilderSpace programme — the hackathon tour and its online onboarding nights — is listed on the events calendar, with every event linked to its Luma page. The channel and the calendar serve different purposes: the channel is for watching, the calendar is for showing up. Both are public, both are honest about what is decided and what is not, and neither asks for money.
